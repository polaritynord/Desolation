Alpha 1.3
* Bullets now have a chance to pierce through NPCs and props. Each weapon has a different chance.
* NPC's now can collide with each other, and can be pushed around.
* In infinite mode, you now get 10 points for surviving every 10 seconds.
* Added a new "Shiny Menu" option in the video settings.
* Added new resolution and fullscreen options to the video settings.
* Fixed some sound errors regarding "hurtme" and "hurtarmor" commands in the developer console.
* Campaign, Changelog and About buttons now work in the main menu (despite not having menus)
* Added new developer console functions:
    * summon [name]: Spawns an NPC in front of the player.
    * newitem [name]: Spawns an item in front of the player.
    * newprop [name]: Spawns a prop in front of the player.
    * tp [x] [y]: Teleports the player to the given coordinates.
* Items now despawn after 4 minutes. (Can be disabled in the json file of the map)
* Medkit and batteries now have a custom pickup sound. (Just used the Black Mesa SFX for now, but will change it later)
* Two new achievements added:
    * "For a Reason": obtained when all the other achievements are obtained.
    * "Play All Radios": unused & unobtainable. Meant for the campaign mode of the game.
* Achievements menu now has a progress text. (might improve it later)
* Hitting ESC now works as a shortcut to "Return" buttons in some menus.
